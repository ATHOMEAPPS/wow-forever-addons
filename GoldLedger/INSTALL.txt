GoldLedger 1.2 - install on another computer
============================================

Works on: the World of Warcraft "_forever_" client (interface 16001).

1. Close World of Warcraft.

2. Copy the whole "GoldLedger" folder (next to this file, with
   GoldLedger.toc, Core.lua, Tracker.lua and UI.lua inside) into:

     C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\

   (Or unzip GoldLedger.zip into that AddOns folder.)

3. Start WoW. At character select click "AddOns":
     - turn ON  GoldLedger
     - turn OFF AllemanoLedger (if that computer has it)

4. Log in. The coin counter appears near the top of the screen.
   Click it, or type /gl, to open the Ledger.

Notes
-----
- Records start from the moment you install. A new computer starts empty.
- Data lives in _forever_\WTF\Account\<ACCOUNT>\SavedVariables\GoldLedger.lua
- /gl            open or close the Ledger
  /gl counter    show or hide the counter
  /gl reset      reset the counter
  /gl options    open the options (also Options > AddOns > GoldLedger)
  /gl errors     show recent errors
