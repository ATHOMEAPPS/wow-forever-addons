Ledger 2.1 - install on another computer
========================================
Made for the Family, by Claude and EonJoe.

Works on: the World of Warcraft "_forever_" client (interface 16001).
Ledger was called GoldLedger up to version 1.3.

1. Close World of Warcraft.

2. Copy the whole "Ledger" folder (next to this file, with
   Ledger.toc, Core.lua, Tracker.lua, Loot.lua, UI.lua and Options.lua
   inside) into:

     C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\

   (Or unzip Ledger.zip into that AddOns folder.)

3. If that computer has the old GoldLedger folder in AddOns, delete it.
   Both must never be on at once, or every coin is counted twice.
   To keep its history, copy
     _forever_\WTF\Account\<ACCOUNT>\SavedVariables\GoldLedger.lua
   to Ledger.lua in the same folder, open Ledger.lua in Notepad, and
   change the line "GoldLedgerDB = {" to "LedgerDB = {".

4. Start WoW. At character select click "AddOns":
     - turn ON  Ledger
     - turn OFF AllemanoLedger (if that computer has it)

5. Log in. The coin counter appears near the top of the screen.
   Click it, or type /ledger, to open the Ledger.

Notes
-----
- Records start from the moment you install. A new computer starts empty.
- Gold: where every coin came from and went, by day, week and month.
- Loot: what each mob dropped and what it sold for. Type an item
  (for example silk) in Search to see which mob drops the most of it.
- Data lives in _forever_\WTF\Account\<ACCOUNT>\SavedVariables\Ledger.lua
- /ledger           open or close the Ledger (/gl works too)
  /ledger loot      open the loot table
  /ledger clear     clear data older than 1, 3, 6 or 12 months
  /ledger counter   show or hide the counter
  /ledger reset     reset the counter
  /ledger options   open the options (also Options > AddOns > Ledger)
  /ledger errors    show recent errors
